Oman
Oman
Government-led, investment-oriented model

Policy and regulation sit with the Ministry of Energy and Minerals (MEM), while the state holds its energy assets through two owners. Energy Oman (EO), a 100% government-owned company set up through the Ministry of Finance (MOF), holds the core upstream resources: 60% of Block 6 (operated by PDO), the Block 6 gas concession and Hydrom. The Oman Investment Authority (OIA), the sovereign wealth fund, owns OQ, the integrated energy group.

Qatar
Single state energy champion model

Its President and CEO is also the Minister of State for Energy Affairs, so policy and operations sit with the same person. Kuwait
Kuwait
Council-led model with one state oil group

Oil policy is set by the Supreme Petroleum Council, chaired by the Prime Minister. Kuwait Petroleum Corporation (KPC), wholly state-owned and chaired by the Oil Minister, runs the whole oil and gas chain through its subsidiaries, which it is merging into fewer companies. Power and water sit with a separate ministry, and new plants are built through public-private partnerships.
